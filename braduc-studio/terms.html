Terms of Service

Last updated: October 9, 2026.

"BRADUC Studio Publisher" is an automated content publishing tool operated by BRADUC Studio. It is used to publish short videos produced by BRADUC Studio on the studio's own social media accounts.

What the app does

  • Uploads finished videos for publication on the connected accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok), using each platform's official API.
  • Does not publish on behalf of third parties and is not offered as a service to other users.
  • Does not access, collect or process data of other platform users.

Use

API access is limited to the studio's own accounts, explicitly authorized through each platform's official OAuth flow. Content published through the app follows the community guidelines and terms of each platform.

Contact

Questions about these terms: braduc.studio@gmail.com.

O acesso via API é limitado às contas do próprio estúdio, autorizadas explicitamente pelo fluxo OAuth oficial de cada plataforma. O conteúdo publicado pelo aplicativo segue as diretrizes da comunidade e os termos de cada plataforma.
